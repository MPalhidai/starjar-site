Star Jar is a simple, cheerful reward jar. Set a goal, then drag a star (or a marble) into the jar each time one is earned. When the jar is full, it’s time to celebrate! Great for kids’ chores, new habits, and good behavior.

Why you’ll like it

⭐

Stars or marbles

Pick shiny stars or glossy marbles, and set any goal from 1 to 50.

🎉

A happy finish

Every star earns a “Great Job!” with confetti, and a full jar says “Time for your reward!”

🔒

Private by design

No ads, accounts, or tracking, and the iPhone app works offline. Your jar is saved only on your own device.

How it works

  1. Set a goal. Tap Goal and choose how many stars fill the jar.
  2. Add a star. Each time one is earned, drag a star into the jar (or swipe to add).
  3. Made a mistake? Drag a star back out, or swipe left on the jar to remove one.
  4. Celebrate! When the jar is full, collect the reward and start a fresh jar.
