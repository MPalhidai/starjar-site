Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Star Jar is a simple reward jar: you drag stars or marbles into a jar until you reach a goal. This policy covers the Star Jar iPhone app, the Star Jar web app at star-jar-gules.vercel.app, and this website. Star Jar is made by Michael Palhidai (“we”, “us”).

What we collect

Nothing. Star Jar does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and the app makes no network calls.

What is stored on your device

Your jar — how many items are in it, your goal, and whether you chose stars or marbles — is saved only on your own device using local storage, so it is still there the next time you open the app. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app (or clearing the website’s data in your browser) removes it.

Third parties

Star Jar does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and does not share data with anyone. If you download the app from Apple’s App Store, Apple’s own privacy policy applies to the App Store itself.

Children

Star Jar is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

This website

starjar.me is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the pages; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contacting us

If you email us at support@starjar.me, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@starjar.me.
